# Privacy Policy — MQTT Scout

**Last updated:** June 2025

MQTT Scout does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

- No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- Broker hostnames, ports, credentials, and preferred-broker settings are stored **only on your device** (local app storage via Capacitor Preferences).
- MQTT traffic goes directly between your device and the broker you choose; we do not operate intermediary servers.

If you have questions, open an issue at
https://github.com/mhaberler/mdns-mqtt-vue3
